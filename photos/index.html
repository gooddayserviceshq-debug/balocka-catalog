How I handle photos of kids
  • Galleries show jersey numbers only — never a player's name.
  • Everything is public and self-serve. I don't send photos of a specific child to anyone privately, because there's no way to know who is asking.
  • Don't send me photos of your kids. I don't collect or repost family photos.
  • I shoot from the sideline in public view, with the school's knowledge.
Want a photo taken down? Tell any Smyrna coach or athletic staff member, or message me, and it's gone that day. No explanation needed and I won't ask. Want your child left out of galleries entirely? Say so once and that's permanent.

Common questions

Is there really no charge?

No charge, ever. Players and families never pay. Organizations with budgets hire me — that's how this works.

Can I post them?

Please do. A tag (@balocka_creative) is appreciated, never required.

I want a bigger print

Gallery files print well to 8×10. For anything larger, ask your coach or team parent to pass along the request and I'll put a bigger file in the gallery.

My athlete isn't in the gallery

I shoot more than I post. Pass the jersey number through your coach or team parent and I'll add what I have to the gallery.
