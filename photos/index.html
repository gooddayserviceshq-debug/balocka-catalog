Common questions

Is there really no charge?

No charge. Players and families never pay me. Organizations with budgets hire me — that's how this works.

Can I post them?

Please do. A tag (@balocka_creative) is appreciated but never required.

Can I get a print?

I'll send you a print-ready file and you can print it anywhere. If you want something big or framed, message me and I'll help you size it right.

My kid isn't in the gallery

Tell me the jersey number. I shoot far more than I post, and there's a good chance I have shots that didn't make the public gallery.

I don't want my child's photo online

Just tell me and I'll pull it, no questions asked. You can still get your own copies privately.

Want to send something back? Send me a photo of you with your athlete — the ones I can't take, in the stands or after the game — and I'll send back a Player Pack: hand-edited shots, a phone wallpaper crop, and a print file. How the swap works
