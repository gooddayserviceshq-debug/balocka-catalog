Where the credit goes

  • Instagram / Facebook — anywhere in the caption is fine, or tag @balocka_creative in the post. A tag alone counts; you don't need both.
  • Website — a caption under the photo, or a photo-credit line at the bottom of the page.
  • Print / flyers — small text along the edge of the image is plenty.
  • Video or slideshow — one mention in the end credits covers every photo used.

What you can do without asking

Yes, go ahead
  • Post on any Town social account, as often as you want
  • Use on the Town website, newsletters, and email
  • Crop, resize, or adjust brightness to fit your layout
  • Print for flyers, banners, displays, or recap boards
  • Send to local press covering the event
  • Re-use next year to promote the event again
Just check with me first
  • A business or sponsor wants them for their own advertising
  • Anything sold commercially
  • Heavy edits that change what actually happened at the event
Not a problem in any of these cases — I'd just like to know. A text is enough.
