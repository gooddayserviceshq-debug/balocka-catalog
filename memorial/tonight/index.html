You have no flash

Stand where the light already is. That's the whole technique tonight. Find the brightest spot at the finish — light pole, timing tent, doorway — and shoot everyone crossing through it. Put the light behind you, falling on their faces.

Tone — it's a memorial

  • Moment of silence: one frame, no burst. Shutter noise carries.
  • Lower the camera if someone is grieving. Ask before close portraits of family.
  • Celebration is fine at the finish. Restraint during the ceremony.
  • Watch for the number 6 — Kuss flew Blue Angel 6. That's why it's a 6K.
