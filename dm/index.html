Instagram auto-reply — set this up in 5 minutes

Option A — Keyword auto-reply (best)

Needs a Professional account (Business or Creator — free to switch).

  1. Instagram app → Settings
  2. Business tools and controls → Saved replies
  3. Create a reply with shortcut photos — paste the text below
  4. Then Settings → Business → Frequently asked questions — add "How do I get my photos?" so it shows as a tappable button on your profile

In a DM, type photos and the full reply drops in. Two taps.

Option B — Away message (instant, no setup)

Settings → Business tools → Away message. Turn it on during games and event nights. Everyone who DMs gets the instructions automatically while you're shooting.

Option C — Link in bio

Put gooddayserviceshq-debug.github.io/balocka-catalog/photos/ in your bio as "Get your photos free." Then most DMs never need a reply at all.
